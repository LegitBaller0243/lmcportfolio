Overview (100-150 words)

This synopsis summarizes the rhetorical investigation into the 2018 Project Maven crisis at Google, where a lack of transparency regarding a Department of Defense contract led to a significant internal revolt. The project consists of a detailed technical incident report analyzing the breakdown of internal trust and a public-facing infographic designed to translate these ethical complexities for a general audience. The research highlights how the mismatch between leadership’s bureaucratic framing and the employees' moral concerns created a communicative rift that ultimately forced a total shift in corporate policy. As a technical communicator, I made several strategic choices to navigate this complex issue. I prioritized audience adaptation by pivoting from a formal, specialized tone in the report to a highly visual, accessible narrative in the infographic. In the report, I utilized rhetorical analysis to dissect primary sources like the "Googlers Against Maven" petition, focusing on how specific language choices impacted stakeholder trust. For the infographic, I chose a non-linear design featuring an information flow graphic and a crisis timeline, ensuring the technical details were digestible for non-specialists while maintaining the ethical gravity of the situation.
