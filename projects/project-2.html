Overview (100-150 words)

In this project, I produced a technical crisis report and an accompanying infographic that translate complex information into clear, actionable communication for a public-facing audience. The report focuses on analytical depth, source-based reasoning, and structured recommendations, while the infographic distills key findings into a visual format that supports quick comprehension. Together, these deliverables demonstrate how I balance detail with accessibility across different modes. I made deliberate choices in organization, emphasis, and design to guide readers through risk, impact, and response priorities. This project shows my ability to communicate urgency without sacrificing accuracy or audience usability across multiple stakeholder contexts effectively.
