Overview (100-150 words)

The Bloomberg Terminal Training materials introduce Georgia Tech students to Bloomberg as a professional financial research platform and guide them from basic orientation to applied workflows. The training explains what Bloomberg is, why it matters in finance-related fields, and how students can use command-based navigation, keyboard shortcuts, company pages, news tools, and comparable company analysis to gather and interpret financial information. I designed the material for beginners by using plain language, short sections, screenshots, captions, key takeaways, and repeated “what you are doing / why this matters” explanations. These choices reduce cognitive overload and help students understand not just which commands to enter, but the purpose behind each step. I also tailored the content to student users by connecting Bloomberg tasks to recognizable examples, practical finance workflows, and post-workflow interpretation using Claude.
