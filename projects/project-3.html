Overview (100-150 words)

This project includes community training materials and a recommendation report designed to support practical decision-making and user learning. I developed the training content to be easy to navigate, instruction-focused, and adaptable for audiences with different prior knowledge levels. The recommendation report complements the training by presenting rationale, evidence, and suggested improvements in a concise, professional format. Across both deliverables, I focused on clarity, sequencing, and reader support so users can move from information to action. This project demonstrates my ability to combine instructional communication with analytical reporting and to design content that is both informative and usable in real settings.
