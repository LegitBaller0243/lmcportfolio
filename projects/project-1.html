Overview (100-150 words)

This project contains the core career materials I developed to present myself to professional audiences: a targeted resume and a tailored cover letter. I treated both documents as technical communication artifacts, not just job paperwork, by focusing on audience expectations, clear information hierarchy, and concise language. I revised wording, formatting, and emphasis so each section quickly communicates value to a recruiter or hiring manager. The final set reflects my ability to adapt tone and content for purpose, align evidence with role requirements, and design documents that are scannable, credible, and persuasive in high-stakes professional contexts for competitive applications and interviews.
