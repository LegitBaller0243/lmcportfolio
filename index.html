Introduction

My name is Sameer Jivangikar. I am a software engineer and researcher focused on the intersection of high-performance computing, systems programming, and AI infrastructure. Currently pursuing my BS in Computer Science at Georgia Tech, I specialize in developing efficient, scalable systems—from custom CUDA kernel optimization and GPU memory hierarchy management to complex fluid simulations. I thrive at the level where software meets hardware, whether I'm optimizing a performance-critical simulation or building the infrastructure for the next generation of "agentic" systems.

Beyond engineering, I’ve realized that the most impactful technical work is only as strong as its ability to be understood. Throughout my LMC 3403 course, I’ve learned how to pivot my communication style to ensure complex information can be interpreted by non-technical audiences without losing its core value. By applying specific rhetorical and design principles across various projects and assignments this semester, I’ve shifted from just "reporting data" to intentionally crafting narratives for different stakeholders. This portfolio reflects that journey, demonstrating how I bridge the gap between deep technical execution and accessible, purposeful communication.
