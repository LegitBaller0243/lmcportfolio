Overview (100-150 words)

This project, KLR.ai, was created as an end-to-end technical assignment to demonstrate how AI can turn raw visual data into practical, user-facing guidance. The goal was to build more than a model demo: I designed a full pipeline that detects objects (YOLO), estimates relative depth (MiDaS), and applies spatial risk logic to identify which obstacles are most important in a scene. That structured output is then transformed into prompt-ready data for an LLM to generate concise, accessible responses.

For outside audiences, this work illustrates both engineering and communication strength. I translated complex computer vision and depth concepts into explainable system behavior, documented tradeoffs clearly, and designed outputs that non-technical users can understand. It reflects how I approach technical communication: accurate, audience-aware, and grounded in real product decisions.
